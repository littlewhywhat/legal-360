# Room → Mission — desktop

Two desks on one screen: Mira (English) and Lea (Czech). Each desk starts on that person's mission list. Lea's chrome, steps, titles, todos, and artifacts are in Czech.

Filters: QQ (push), DQ (in the list, no push), Stakeholder (on the mission, not waiting).

A room is the team space: shared context, stakeholders, agent pool, the missions in that room. The pool is on the room, not on the mission.

Mission: playbook and todos side by side. Stakeholder threads sit in tabs — one conversation at a time, composer only on your own tab. Artifact files stay on the right.
