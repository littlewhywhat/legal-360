# Room → Mission — desktop

Three desks on one screen: Product owner, UX, Dev. Each desk starts on that person's mission list.

Filters: QQ (push), DQ (in the list, no push), Stakeholder (on the mission, not waiting).

A room is the team space: shared context, stakeholders, agent pool, the missions in that room. The pool is on the room, not on the mission.

Mission: playbook and todos on top, every stakeholder's thread in the center (own thread can be typed, the others are read-only), artifact files on the right.
