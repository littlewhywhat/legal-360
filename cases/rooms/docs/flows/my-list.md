# Rooms — desktop

Home is My list. Filters are QQ, DQ, and In play. A room name on a row opens that room. The same rooms sit in the sidebar.

A room shows its stakeholders, the agent pool, and its missions. A mission row names the person on the gate or the bot on the work.

A mission has a playbook, artifacts, and one stakeholder thread at a time. The prompt field types itself. The pool stays on the room.

New mission: pick a room and a playbook. The room then shows the mission and moves a free agent onto it. A later checkpoint can be inserted. Close is Done or Garbage.
